Ephemeris

Ephemeris is a Dynamic skydome system A “drag and drop” solution to implementing our Skydome.

It supports various platforms and can be integrated into any custom engine.

I have implemented Ephemeris for over 6 months.

And, Stormland from Insomniac Games uses customized version of it as their Volumetric Clouds system.

You can also check the demo video with clicking the images below.

Main Features

  • Dynamic 24 hour time cycle (sunrises, sunsets, blue sky, sun and moon)
  • Atmospheric Scattering
  • Volumetric Clouds
  • God rays
  • 2.5D Clouds for mobile (Coming Soon!)
  • Simulation of astronomically correct sun, stars and moon
